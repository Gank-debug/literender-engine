LiteRender for Mac (Apple silicon)

1. Unzip, then drag LiteRender.app into Applications (or anywhere you like).
2. Open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the developer:
   click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down
   and click "Open Anyway" next to LiteRender, and confirm. After that it
   opens normally. (No Terminal needed.)

Using it
  * With SketchUp running, the app finds it and loads the open model; edits
    update live. First time: click "Set up SketchUp link" (top right), then
    restart SketchUp.
  * Or open a .lrscene file (Extensions > LiteRender > Export .lrscene).
